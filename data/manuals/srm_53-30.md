## SRM 53-30-01 — Cracks, fuselage skin (ATA 53), aluminium 2024-T3

Allowable damage limits for surface cracks in the pressurised fuselage skin:
- Up to 2.0 mm: within limits. No action beyond recording in the structural log and
  re-inspection at the next scheduled check.
- 2.0 mm to 6.0 mm: major damage. A repair must be scheduled. Continued service is
  permitted under a damage-tolerance assessment until repair.
- 6.0 mm to 12.0 mm: critical. Repair before the crack reaches the 12.0 mm limit.
  Inspection interval must be shortened per the damage-tolerance analysis.
- Above 12.0 mm: beyond allowable limit. The aircraft is NOT airworthy in this
  condition. A certified repair scheme must be embodied before return to service.

Repair scheme: external doubler per SRM 53-30-01 Fig. 4, fastener pattern as table 3.

## SRM 53-30-05 — Corrosion, fuselage skin (ATA 53)

Pitting corrosion up to 0.5 mm depth is within limits. Between 0.5 mm and 3.0 mm,
blend-out and treat per SRM 51-20. Beyond 8.0 mm equivalent material loss the panel
must be repaired or replaced before return to service.

## General

Every finding in ATA 53 must be recorded against the tail number with its measured
size, location (station / stringer / frame), and inspection cycle count. Only
certifying staff may approve a return to service.
