AeroTwin-Edge — 2-MINUTE VIRTUAL POC SCRIPT
Record screen with OBS Studio (free); film the Pi on a phone; cut in DaVinci
Resolve or CapCut (free). Total ~120 s. Time-coded [SCREEN] / [SAY].

CONSISTENCY RULE: the cycle number you SAY here, the number on the dashboard
chart, and the number on SLIDE 4/8 must all match. This script uses ~40 cycles;
twin_console.py uses PROJECTED_CYCLES = 40. Change all three together if you
adjust it. (See POC_BUILD_RUNBOOK.txt — note on the 40-cycle figure.)

DELIVERY TIPS
- One continuous take per segment; calm, declarative voice.
- Show the mm number and the SRM limit on screen long enough to read.
- Do NOT rush the detection moment (0:30-0:55) — that is the credibility shot.
- Record one clean end-to-end backup take and keep it on disk before the real one.
