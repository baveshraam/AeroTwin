================================================================
SLIDE 1 — PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================
Aircraft structural maintenance is scheduled on a fixed CALENDAR / hard-time
interval — a blind clock that is wrong in two expensive directions at once:

- OVER-maintenance. The healthy majority of the fleet is pulled off-line for
  checks it did not need — wasted downtime, labour and parts.
- UNDER-maintenance. The few tails with a fast-growing crack or advancing
  corrosion can outrun the interval and reach an SRM limit BETWEEN scheduled
  checks — the safety and AOG tail-risk (~ Rs 8 lakh-1.25 crore per grounded hr).

Root cause: there is NO continuous, per-tail condition record to schedule
against. Findings sit on paper task cards and siloed systems, so "did this crack
grow since the last C-check on THIS tail?" is genuinely hard to answer — and
severity is still judged against SRM limits by eye, so the measurement that feeds
any decision isn't even repeatable.

And cloud can't fix it: ITAR-restricted hangars are network-isolated and imagery
legally cannot leave the site. The missing piece is a digital twin, held at the edge.

================================================================
SLIDE 2 — OBJECTIVE
================================================================
Deliver an offline, edge-resident structural DIGITAL TWIN on a Raspberry
Pi-class device that maintains, per tail and across the fleet:

1. A condition record — every inspection appended, severity-graded against SRM
   allowable-damage limits (a repeatable mm measurement, not an eyeball).
2. A twin state — a health index (0-100) per aircraft, remaining useful life in
   flight cycles, and a CONDITION-BASED scheduling call: PULL the check FORWARD on
   a fast grower, SAFELY EXTEND it on a healthy jet, GROUND a beyond-limit one.
3. A fleet risk board — every tail ranked worst-first, so the morning planner
   sees at a glance which aircraft needs attention and which is safe to defer.
4. A cited, human-signed work order + a tamper-evident audit trail for the regulator.

Goal: replace blind calendar maintenance with condition-based maintenance (CBM+)
— fully offline and ITAR-compliant. Defect detection is one sensor feeding this twin.

================================================================
SLIDE 3 — APPROACH
================================================================
Edge-first, because the domain forces it:
- Hangars are RF-restricted and often network-dead.
- ITAR/EAR/data-sovereignty: OEM and defence imagery cannot hit public cloud.
- AOG economics: a verdict in seconds at the aircraft beats an upload loop.

Build one narrow, flawless vertical slice; architect the rest:
- BUILT (L0-L3): edge detect + segment + mm severity grade -> per-tail twin ->
  Paris-law progression -> agentic, manual-cited work order.
- ROADMAP (L4-L6): federated fleet learning, closed-loop MRO ops, AR overlay.

Data-scarcity answer (built in, not bolted on): self-supervised backbones +
unsupervised anomaly detection + synthetic defects + active learning, so we do
not depend on thousands of labelled aerospace defect images.

================================================================
SLIDE 4 — SOLUTION OVERVIEW
================================================================
AeroTwin-Edge — a structural digital twin driving Condition-Based MRO at the edge.

The unbroken chain (offline on the Pi):
  Inspection of a skin panel  (camera today; borescope / eddy-current later)
    -> SENSE + GRADE   "ATA 53, stringer 14, crack 8.0 mm — MAJOR (67% of limit)"
    -> TWIN            "tail VT-AXB, was 6.0 mm last C-check, +2.0 mm; health 33/100"
    -> PREDICT         "remaining useful life ~N flight cycles to the 12 mm SRM limit"
    -> DECIDE          "PULL INSPECTION FORWARD — growth outruns the calendar interval"
    -> FLEET BOARD     4 tails ranked: 1 GROUNDED, 2 PULL-FORWARD, 1 SAFE-TO-EXTEND 30k cyc
    -> AGENT           drafts a work order citing SRM 53-xx; certifying staff sign
  Hash-chain logged. Pi in airplane mode. Verdict in seconds.

Not "an AI that spots cracks" — the digital twin that decides, per tail AND across
the fleet, what to fix, when, on which aircraft, and proves it to the regulator.

SLIDE 5 — TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================
IMPLEMENTED (this POC):
- DIGITAL TWIN BRAIN (the hero): per-tail health index (0-100) from worst open
  defect vs its SRM limit; remaining-useful-life = min cycles-to-limit across open
  defects; condition-based scheduling decision (PULL FORWARD / SAFE TO EXTEND /
  GROUND) quantified against the calendar baseline; FLEET RISK BOARD ranking every
  tail worst-first. (src/l1_twin/twin.py — pure, unit-tested logic.)
- Twin record: per-tail SQLite store; findings pinned to ATA zone / station / stringer.
- Sensor (detection/segmentation): YOLO segmentation with SAHI tiling for thin,
  small, high-aspect-ratio cracks (pixel-accurate masks, not coarse boxes).
- Severity/sizing: OpenCV + ArUco fiducial -> pixels to millimetres, graded against
  a configurable SRM allowable-damage-limit table.
- Progression: Paris-law + empirical two-scan growth -> cycles-to-limit.
- Agentic copilot: retrieval-grounded drafting over an AMM/SRM excerpt corpus,
  citation-required, human sign-off gate.
- Audit: hash-chained, tamper-evident decision log (verify() re-walks the chain).
- Edge runtime: offline execution on Raspberry Pi 5; latency shown on screen.

ARCHITECTED (roadmap):
- Unsupervised anomaly detection (PatchCore / EfficientAD) for unknown defects.
- INT8 quantization + pruning/distillation; optional Hailo-8L accelerator.
- Federated learning (Flower / FedAvg) with secure aggregation + differential
  privacy — models improve across the fleet; raw imagery never leaves the site.
- Hash-chained, tamper-evident audit trail; signed models; OTA registry.

Stack: PyTorch, Ultralytics/YOLO, OpenCV, ONNX Runtime / TFLite / NCNN,
FastAPI/Streamlit, SQLite, a small local LLM for RAG. Bootstrap data: NEU-DET,
SDNET2018, GAPs + synthetic defects.

SLIDE 6 — CHALLENGES FACED
================================================================
- Data scarcity (no large aerospace defect corpus)
    -> unsupervised anomaly detection + synthetic generation + transfer + active
       learning, designed in from the start.
- Thin, tiny cracks evade naive detectors
    -> SAHI high-resolution tiling + a segmentation head for accurate geometry.
- Pixels are not airworthiness
    -> ArUco fiducial calibration to millimetres, with stated measurement
       uncertainty (gauge R&R mindset).
- Edge compute ceiling (Pi 5 has no built-in NPU)
    -> INT8 quantization, pruning, distillation; optional Hailo-8L; "minimum
       resource" is the thesis, not a compromise.
- LLM hallucination is unacceptable in a safety context
    -> citation-required guardrail; the agent refuses and escalates rather than
       inventing a limit; certifying-staff sign-off gate.
- Certification credibility
    -> decision-support framing, audit trail, and named standards (EASA ML
       learning assurance, SAE G-34/AS6983) — building for the audit from day one.
